# JomLuah Landing Page Direction

## Identity

JomLuah gives UTHM students a private place to reflect with an AI companion and journal.

## Personality and mood

Gentle, clear, and reassuring. Keep the page calm enough for students arriving with difficult feelings, while making the conversation preview feel present and human.

## Palette and type

- Use the sampled violet-blue (#563bfa) as the primary accent, over a soft white-to-light-blue background with neutral surfaces.
- Keep the violet-blue for the main action and conversation cues. Use neutrals for supporting content.
- Use the existing Plus Jakarta Sans typeface for a familiar, readable voice.

## Composition and motion

- ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.
- Give the headline and chat preview equal weight in the opening section, matching the approved left-copy/right-conversation layout.
- Let the companion feature lead. Present journal writing and mood reflection with distinct treatments that fit their content.
- Use the typing sequence to demonstrate a chat exchange. Provide a pause control and respect reduced-motion preference.

## Purpose notes

- The sampled violet-blue identifies primary actions and chat messages.
- The asymmetrical hero gives the actual companion interaction a visible place beside the introduction.
- The journal sample uses a paper-like surface to distinguish personal writing from chat.
- The mood slider uses a single brand color so interaction state stays legible without adding unrelated colors.
